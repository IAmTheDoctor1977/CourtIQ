# Court IQ

A pickleball rating tool for league and tournament organizers who don't want to rely on DUPR or self-rating.

**Live app:** https://iamthedoctor1977.github.io/CourtIQ/

## How it works

1. **Scenario test (court IQ).** The organizer hands the player a phone or tablet. The player works through 25 animated game situations (5 per level, 3.0 to 5.0+), choosing the best and worst of four options and rating their confidence. The player never sees scores or answer keys.
2. **Skills check (execution).** The organizer observes and marks 50 doubles skills (10 per level, 3.0 to 4.5 from Drill Lab, plus 5.0). The app opens the player's court IQ level and the level above for full testing, with spot checks below.
3. **Rating.** The final rating is the lower of the skills level and the court IQ level + 0.5, in quarter steps (e.g. 4.75).
4. **Report.** Download a one-page report for the player, or copy a short summary into an email. Reports show results by domain and skill, never scenario text or keys.

## Data

Player records are stored in the browser on the organizer's device. Use **Settings → Download backup** regularly; restore the file to move records to another device.

## Status

Pilot. Answer keys, skill thresholds and cut scores are draft values to be confirmed by experienced players and calibrated against players with known ratings.
